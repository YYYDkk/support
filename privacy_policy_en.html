2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to the App and the NewQ-B3 functions described in the applicable user manual, including local file management, photo backup, contact backup, camera-related local saving, access to attached SD/USB storage, local device status display, and local network/device management.

3. No NewQ Account Required for Core B3 Functions

The core local functions of the NewQ-B3 described above do not require a NewQ user account. We do not operate a NewQ cloud service for storing your photos, videos, music, documents, contacts, B3 configuration data, or continuous B3 usage telemetry as part of these core functions.

4. Information Processed by the App

4.1 Files Selected by You

When you actively use file management, mobile-phone backup, photo backup, or similar functions, the App accesses only the files and folders necessary for the action you request, subject to the permissions granted on your mobile device.

  • Examples include photos, videos, music, documents, and other files selected for copying, backup, restore, viewing, or management.
  • These files are transferred locally to or from storage media connected to the NewQ-B3, such as an SD card, USB flash drive, HDD, or SSD.
  • The Company does not upload or retain copies of these user files on a NewQ-operated cloud server.

4.2 Contacts

If you actively use the contact backup or restore function, the App may request access to contacts through the permission system of your mobile operating system.

  • Contact data is used only to perform the backup or restore action requested by you.
  • Backed-up contact data is stored on the storage medium selected by you.
  • The Company does not upload or retain copies of your contact backup on a NewQ-operated cloud server.
  • If you deny the contacts permission, the contact backup/restore function may not work, while unrelated App functions can remain available.

4.3 Local Network and Device Information

To communicate with the NewQ-B3 over the local network, the App may locally process technical information needed to establish and display the connection, such as the B3 device name, connection status, storage capacity/available capacity, network mode, transfer status, and similar local operational information. This information is used to provide the requested B3 functions and is not continuously uploaded to a NewQ telemetry cloud.

4.4 App Cache

The App may store cache data locally on your mobile device to support normal operation and faster display of locally available information. Cache data remains stored until you use the App's cache-clearing function, uninstall the App, or reset the mobile device, depending on the operating system and device behavior.

4.5 Crash and Stability Diagnostics Provided by Apple or Google

On iOS, Apple may provide app crash statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. On Android, Google may provide app quality and stability information through Google Play Console, including Android Vitals.

  • These are platform-native diagnostic functions operated by Apple or Google.
  • We do not operate a separate NewQ cloud telemetry or crash-analysis server for the B3 App.
  • The exact diagnostic data made available, its retention period, and related processing are determined by Apple or Google and may depend on platform settings and user choices.
  • Where these reports are made available to us, we use them for app stability, troubleshooting, and product improvement.

4.6 Information You Provide to Customer Support

If you contact us voluntarily, we may process the information included in your message, such as your email address, order information, device information, screenshots, videos, or other troubleshooting information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request, provide support, and diagnose the reported issue.

5. Permissions

The App may request system permissions that are necessary for specific user-initiated functions, including access to photos/media/files, contacts, camera, or local network features, depending on your operating system and App version. You can manage permissions through your mobile device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related function from operating but does not automatically prevent unrelated functions from being used.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases

Where the GDPR or similar data-protection law applies, we process personal data only where a valid legal basis is available. Depending on the situation, this may include:

  • Performance of a contract or user request: to provide the App functions or customer support requested by you.
  • Legitimate interests: to maintain App stability, diagnose technical problems, prevent misuse, and improve product reliability, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Consent: where the operating system or applicable law requires your consent for a specific permission or processing activity.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and do not use your B3 user files for advertising.

Data may be processed by third parties only where relevant to the service you use, including:

  • Apple: for iOS/App Store platform functions and platform-native crash/stability diagnostics.
  • Google: for Android/Google Play platform functions and platform-native crash/stability diagnostics, including Android Vitals.
  • Service providers used for support or communications: only where necessary to deliver the relevant service and subject to applicable data-protection requirements.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Your user-selected files and contact backups are not uploaded to Apple, Google, or a NewQ cloud by the B3 backup function itself. This statement does not limit independent operating-system or platform processing that may occur under Apple or Google policies.

8. Data Retention

  • User files and contact backups: remain on the storage medium chosen by you until you delete, overwrite, or format them.
  • App cache: remains locally until cleared in the App, the App is uninstalled, or the mobile device is reset, as applicable.
  • Customer-support communications: are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain appropriate support records, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Apple/Google diagnostics: retention is governed by the relevant platform and is not controlled by a NewQ-operated diagnostic cloud.

9. International Data Processing

The Company is based in China. If you contact us for support, information you voluntarily provide may be received and processed by the Company in China. Apple and Google may process platform diagnostic information in locations determined by their own services and policies. Where international transfers of personal data are subject to applicable data-protection requirements, they will be handled in accordance with those requirements.

10. Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we process. Because the B3 is designed primarily for local access, you are also responsible for protecting your mobile device, B3 Wi-Fi credentials, local network access, and connected storage media.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, or withdrawal of consent. These rights apply to personal data that the Company actually controls or processes.

For user files stored only on your own storage media or cache stored only on your mobile device, you normally exercise control directly by deleting or managing the relevant local data.

To exercise a privacy right concerning data held by the Company, contact zhifubao@yongdongke.com. You may also have the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority.

12. Children's Privacy
